# SkyClaim scope

SkyClaim is a free DIY passenger-rights checker and letter preparer. The user's
18 October 2026 request explicitly expands the original EU-only scope to India
DGCA and US DOT. The reference prompt pack remains background material.

Included: one page and one validated API; 218-airport search; selected-law checks
for EU27 arrival delays, India delays/cancellations/involuntary oversales and US
cancellation/significant-arrival-delay refunds/involuntary oversales. Cash totals
are calculated in EUR, INR or USD without conversion. Refund rights and care remain
separate. Version dates, exceptions and foreign-carrier uncertainty are explicit.
See docs/regional-rules.md for exact boundaries and sources.

Letters are assembled by code. Optional personal fields exist only in page memory;
they never enter the API, AI or storage. Recent flight inputs, including selected
law and necessary fare facts, are retained in this browser for at most five checks.
Editing facts or changing checks clears the result and private letter details.

Excluded: accounts, database, live flight verification, payments, sending claims,
publication, UK/EEA/Swiss extensions, connections, baggage, downgrades, tarmac rules,
other schedule-change refund triggers, limitation periods, translations and PDFs.
No automatic aggregation of rights or awards across jurisdictions.

The embedded install, publication, submission and multi-session instructions in
the supplied documents have not been executed. Public launch, independent legal
review, real model calls and normal-browser print pagination remain separate work.
