Follow one flight check.

A direct-flight passenger enters disruption facts, receives a qualified assessment and prepares their own letter. This offline map describes actual code. It is a reference route, not evidence of a completed learner tour.

A three-stop route

  1. The action: find async function submit in app/page.tsx. It validates the form, sends the check, handles cancellation/errors, and stores sanitized flight history.
  2. The decision: assessClaim in lib/assess.ts selects EU, India or US rules. lib/evaluation-contract.ts independently checks returned facts and the letter before display.
  3. The output: ClaimLetter in components/claim-letter.tsx uses the same template for preview/copy/download/print. Personal fields remain in this component's state.

To change this, start here

Landing and mobile layout

components/landing-hero.tsx, components/site-navigation.tsx, app/product.css. Keep examples labelled and calculate their amounts with the real engine.

Legal rules or supported dates

lib/eu261.ts, lib/regional-rules.ts, lib/schema.ts and the corresponding tests. Verify official sources and qualified interpretations before changing results.

Private letter fields and export

components/claim-letter.tsx, lib/letter-details.ts, lib/letter-template.ts. Keep personal details out of server requests, AI and history.

Roadmap and deployment

lib/roadmap.ts holds planned features. docs/firebase-setup.md describes the cloud rollout. The prepared Firebase counter stores aggregate counts and expiry, not passenger cases.

A practice to reuse

Connect a concrete requirement to observable evidence. This project recomputes monetary facts at the response boundary, and its HTTP smoke check tests a genuinely stalled upload. A working page alone does not establish correct results.
