# SkyClaim — continuation scope

This is a dated review of the existing root scope, not a pre-build interview record.
The user authorized completion of the frontend and submission preparation.

## The Unique Kernel

A traveler chooses one passenger-rights framework and receives a qualified
assessment plus a deterministic letter. Cash compensation, refund rights and
assistance remain distinct. Code decides all monetary facts.

## Who It's For

A traveler with a disrupted direct flight who wants to understand their position
and prepare their own request to the operating airline. Actual demand is unvalidated.

## The Core Loop

Open the checker, select a rulebook, enter flight and disruption facts, inspect
the reasoning, and complete/copy/download a draft letter. Revisit a recent check
using flight-only browser history. SkyClaim does not send the request.

## Inspiration & Identity

Retain the supplied paper/navy/brass design, serif headings and case-file layout.
The current frontend extends that visual reference with interactive sample cases.

## What "Working" Looks Like

The EU example gives EUR600 per passenger/EUR1,200 for two, the India cancellation
example gives INR6,500/INR13,000 plus a separate refund assessment, and the US
oversales example gives USD1,600/USD3,200. These are synthetic inputs, not flight records.

## The POC Boundary

EU27 arrival delays; India DGCA delays, cancellations and involuntary oversales;
US DOT cancellation/later-arrival refund checks and involuntary oversales. Direct
flights under one selected law, with conditional outcomes and supported-version dates.

## Later

Legal review, verified live Firebase operation, assistive-technology/print review,
evidence guidance, connections, additional jurisdictions and optional saved cases.

## Explicitly Cut

Live flight verification, automatic sending, payments, case representation,
document uploads and PDFs: these require additional providers, evidence and controls.
