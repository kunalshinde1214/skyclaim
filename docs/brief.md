# Project brief

SkyClaim helps a delayed passenger understand whether an EU261 delay claim is
plausible, see the applicable compensation calculation, and prepare a letter to
the operating airline. It is a free DIY tool for someone with little patience
and no familiarity with passenger-rights law.

The experience should replace uncertainty with a useful next action. It uses plain
English, calm authority, visible calculations, and a letter the passenger controls.
No account or commission is involved. It never contacts the airline for them.

The core trust mechanism is code-owned coverage, distance, compensation bands and
totals. The optional language model can rephrase an explanation; it cannot supply
the compensation demand or letter. Uncertain causation leads to an evidence request.

The supplied "The Verdict" theme is the visual reference: warm paper, navy ink,
serif display typography, brass guides, ledger calculations and verdict stamps.

## Assumptions & Deviations

No unverified market statistics are used. The original British Airways SFO–LHR demo
is replaced by a CDG–JFK EU-departure example. This is an initial implementation,
not a completed separate-session BMAD research pipeline or submission.
